How reliability is computed

Attended sessions as a share of booked sessions over a rolling 20-session window, with late cancellations weighted double.

booked    20
attended  19
late cancel 1 ×2
-----------------
(20 − 2) / 20 = 96%

The 10 Sep cancellation drops out of the window after your 30th session, which takes you back to 99%.
